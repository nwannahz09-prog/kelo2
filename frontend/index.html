The fine
print, up
front.

  • Backer shares are symbolic. They are not stock, not legal equity, and don't register you as an owner of Kelo anywhere.
  • A future revenue-share is a possible arrangement, not an enforceable contract, unless and until it's formalized in writing with proper terms.
  • No financial return is guaranteed. Perks are chances and opportunities, not certainties — and neither is any future payout.
  • Backer and category numbers shown here are live from this prototype's own signups and are not audited by a third party.
  • If revenue-share terms are ever formalized, every backer will be told directly and given the actual agreement to read before anything changes.
  • Payments on this site are a simulated demo. No card is ever charged and no real payment gateway is contacted.
